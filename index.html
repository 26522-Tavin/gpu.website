What Is A GPU?

A Graphics Processing Unit (GPU) is a specialized electronic circuit optimized for high speed memory manipulation and computation. Its primary function is to accelerate the creation, rendering, and display of images, video content, and complex 3D graphics. While originally developed for visual output, modern GPUs are widely utilized for data intensive workloads, including artificial intelligence, machine learning, and scientific simulations.
